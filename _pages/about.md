Welcome to my page! 
======
I am a data scientist, multidisciplinary researcher and math communicator. This site is where I post my explorations with different methods, data sets and analytical tools. You can also find examples of my past communications work on behalf of the Institute for Computational and Experimental Research in Mathematics at Brown University. 

I am open to work and collaborations, particularly combining interesting analytics in education, healthcare or other public service areas. Please feel free to reach out via email with inquiries!
